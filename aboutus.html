Our History

Majority Tech was founded in 2022 to solve a growing problem in modern web development—keeping applications simple for users without compromising backend security. What started as a three-person dev team working out of a shared workspace has grown into a dedicated agency trusted for clean architecture and reliable cloud solutions.

Our Mission

To deliver high-performance, secure digital ecosystems that empower businesses to scale securely and efficiently.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized benchmark for secure software engineering and reliable cloud operations.

Core Values

Security First, Continuous Innovation, Collaborative Growth, and Integrity.
